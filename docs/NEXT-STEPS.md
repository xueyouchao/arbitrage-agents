# Next Steps: Validate Before Further Investment

## 1. Freeze development

For the next 30–45 days:

- Add no venues, strategies, dashboards, or execution features.
- Do not start a separate AI stock-trading project.
- Risk no additional trading capital.
- Set strict spending and time limits that cannot affect household finances.

## 2. Validate the existing scanner

Run the scanner read-only at frequent intervals and record:

- Distinct matched-market pairs.
- Opportunities that survive an immediate order-book refresh.
- Gross and net edge after fees, depth, slippage, and 25 bps adverse selection.
- Executable dollar size.
- Contract-resolution mismatches.
- Estimated capital lock time.

Manually inspect every promising pair. Count repeated observations of the same unchanged opportunity only once.

## 3. Apply hard success criteria

Continue only if, by day 45, the evidence shows:

- A Class A false-positive rate below 5%.
- At least 80% of detected opportunities remain positive at $5 notional.
- Positive median net edge after costs and adverse selection.
- Enough opportunity frequency and executable dollar capacity to justify the operating time.
- No approval of stale, unfillable, or semantically mismatched legs.

## 4. Make the decision

- **Strong results:** Fix fill confirmation and reconciliation, independently review execution safety, and then test tiny, manually approved trades.
- **Positive but economically insignificant results:** Keep the project as analytics or portfolio work, or test demand for an alert product.
- **Few opportunities or negative actionable edge:** End the proprietary-trading objective and archive the project.

## 5. Protect stable income

Regardless of the experiment:

- Prioritize stable employment or contracting.
- Use AI coding tools primarily to increase engineering productivity and income.
- Treat trading as a capped experiment—not a household-income plan—until realized net P&L proves otherwise.
