  My top pick: cross-venue arbitrage

  The best strategy is:

  Find the same or near-identical event listed on multiple venues, buy the underpriced side on one venue, and hedge with the opposite side elsewhere.

  Example:

  Kalshi BTC above $100k YES = 46¢
  Polymarket BTC above $100k NO = 50¢

  Total cost = 96¢
  Max payout = $1
  Locked gross edge = 4¢, before fees/slippage/resolution risk

  This is not glamorous, but it is the cleanest form of prediction-market arbitrage.

  Why I prefer it

  Because it avoids the hardest problem: being right about the future.

  You are mostly betting on:

  - fragmented liquidity
  - different user bases
  - slow price synchronization
  - fee differences
  - capital constraints
  - temporary mispricings
  - UI/API latency between markets

  That is a better game than trying to forecast elections, weather, or crypto direction better than everyone else.

  Main risks

  The risks are real:

  - the two markets may not resolve identically
  - one leg may fail to fill
  - capital can be locked until resolution
  - fees can erase the spread
  - one platform may restrict accounts/API access
  - liquidity can disappear exactly when you need to hedge

  Best prediction topics

  1. Crypto — best for liquidity, not necessarily best for easy profit

  Crypto is probably the best topic for volume and automation.

  Examples:

  - BTC above/below price level
  - ETH price milestones
  - BTC hourly/daily up/down
  - crypto ETF / exchange / liquidation events
  - BTC year-end targets

  Why crypto is attractive

  - trades 24/7
  - data is public and machine-readable
  - high liquidity
  - many platforms list similar markets
  - fast settlement compared with politics
  - easy to model mechanically
  - good for bots

For crypto, I would focus on:

  1. cross-venue price-level markets
  2. 1h / 4h / daily markets, not 15-minute speed war markets
  3. fee-aware market making
  4. stale-market detection after large Binance/Coinbase moves
  - Best use: cross-venue arb + longer-window liquidity dislocations

  2. Macro / economic events — probably the best risk-adjusted topic

  This is my favorite category after crypto.

  Examples:

  - Fed rate decisions
  - CPI prints
  - jobs reports
  - inflation ranges
  - recession indicators
  - central-bank meetings
  - election-adjacent economic policy outcomes

  Why macro is attractive

  - events are clearly scheduled
  - resolution criteria are usually objective
  - liquidity clusters around known times
  - information becomes public all at once
  - markets can overreact or underreact
  - cross-venue listings are common
  - windows last longer than crypto latency windows

  This is where a bot plus human research can work well.

  You can prepare before the event, monitor multiple venues, and exploit stale prices or hedgeable discrepancies.

  Main risks

  - sharp volatility near release
  - partial fills
  - different resolution wording
  - data revisions
  - capital locked until settlement
  - fewer events than crypto

  Honest rating

  - Profit ceiling: Medium–High
  - Competition: Medium
  - Engineering burden: Medium
  - Best use: cross-venue arbitrage, event-window market making, mispriced ranges
